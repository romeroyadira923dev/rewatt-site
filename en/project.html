The project

A response to water acidification in mining areas

ReWATT develops and demonstrates a modular, circular, sustainable and transportable water treatment platform.

Objective

The project aims to remediate mining-impacted environments, including remote sites, while recovering critical raw materials and returning clean water to the environment.

This is a challenge shared by many countries. Acid drainage from mining is increasingly appearing in high-altitude and polar regions, where it threatens fragile ecosystems and exposes vulnerable communities to persistent degradation.

Approach

  • Modularity: a system that adapts to each site.
  • Transportability: can be taken to remote locations.
  • Circularity: raw materials and water recovered within the process.
  • Sustainability: lower environmental impact from remediation and mining.

Fields of work

Geosciences

Characterisation of mining sites and acid drainage.

Environmental engineering

Design and demonstration of the treatment platform.

Chemistry

Selective recovery of critical raw materials from water.

Biotechnology

Biological processes supporting treatment and recovery.

Why it is international

ReWATT runs between Europe and Latin America. Structured, reciprocal staff exchanges between universities, research institutes, industry and public agencies allow partners to share knowledge, technical skills and research infrastructure in real-world settings.
