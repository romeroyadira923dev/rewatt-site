International project · Europe and Latin America

Clean water and raw materials from mining areas

ReWATT treats the acidification of water caused by mining as an opportunity: developing technology to remediate water, recover critical raw materials and return clean water to the environment, including in remote sites.

What we aim to achieve

01

Modular, transportable platform

A water treatment system that can be moved and adapted to remote mining sites.

02

Circular approach

Recover critical raw materials and clean water from the treatment process itself.

03

Real collaboration

Staff exchanges between academia, industry and public agencies to build long-term capacity.

Why it matters

Demand for critical raw materials for the green and digital transitions keeps growing, and the European Union depends heavily on external suppliers. Expanding mining without sustainable solutions could intensify its environmental impact.

ReWATT takes a different path: treating mining-impacted water in a way that reduces damage to vulnerable ecosystems and exposed communities, while recovering the resources it contains.

Next steps

  • Development and demonstration of the treatment platform.
  • Staff exchanges between partners in Europe and Latin America.
  • Shared research infrastructures.
  • Dissemination of results and long-term collaboration.
